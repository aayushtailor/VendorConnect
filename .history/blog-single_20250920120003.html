Top Jewellery Trends in Jaipur 2025

Published on January 20, 2025 • By Jaipur Jewells Team

Jaipur has always been a hub for exquisite jewellery, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern design. In 2025, we’re seeing a beautiful mix of heritage-inspired designs and contemporary minimalism.

1. Polki Revival

Polki jewellery is making a strong comeback, with brides choosing heritage sets that connect with Rajasthani traditions.

2. Minimal Gold Pieces

Lightweight, minimal gold chains and rings are trending among the younger generation who want style with comfort.

3. Sustainable Jewellery

Vendors in Jaipur are now exploring eco-friendly materials and lab-grown diamonds, making sustainability a new luxury.

Whether you’re preparing for a wedding or just want to refresh your collection, Jaipur Jewells connects you with the best vendors to bring these trends to life.
