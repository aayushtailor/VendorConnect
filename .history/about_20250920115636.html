About Jaipur Jewells

Connecting Jaipur’s finest local businesses with customers who value quality, trust, and tradition.

Our Mission

At Jaipur Jewells, our mission is to create a powerful digital platform where local vendors can showcase their work, connect with new customers, and grow their businesses. We aim to make finding reliable services in Jaipur easy, transparent, and trustworthy for everyone.

Our Vision

We envision Jaipur Jewells as the go-to directory for jewellery, fashion, events, and lifestyle vendors in Jaipur. By blending modern technology with local culture, we strive to empower small businesses and create lasting customer relationships.

Frequently Asked Questions

💎 What is Jaipur Jewells?

Jaipur Jewells is a modern online directory that connects customers with trusted local vendors across Jaipur, including jewellers, fashion designers, event planners, and more.

📝 How do I register as a vendor?

Simply visit the Register page, fill in your details, and submit your profile. Once approved, your business will be visible on our platform.

📢 Is registering my business free?

Yes, basic vendor registration is completely free. Premium features and promotions may be available in the future for enhanced visibility.

🔒 How safe is my data?

We take security seriously. All vendor information is stored securely and only shared publicly with your consent.

📞 How can customers contact vendors?

Each vendor profile includes contact details such as phone, WhatsApp, and email, making it easy for customers to reach them directly.

🛠 Can I update my profile later?

Yes, once logged in, you can access your Vendor Dashboard to update your details anytime.
